## Hard / Soft Filters

### Location
- **Accept:** Fully remote US (default pass). A non-mandatory office in Denver or San Diego is a bonus, not a requirement — note it as a plus.
- **Reject:** Mandatory in-office or required-hybrid roles (anywhere, including Denver/SD if attendance is required). Non-US / visa-sponsorship-required roles: flag.

### Salary
- **Floor (hard reject):** anything topping out under $180K total comp.
- **Pass:** $180K+. Target is $200K total comp.
- $200K+: note "excellent salary."
- $180K–200K: note "good salary."
- Undisclosed: include, flag "salary unknown."

### Company Size
- **Hard reject:** over 2,000 employees.
- **Under 1,000:** boost. Smaller is preferred.
- **1,000–2,000:** pass.

### Legacy-Code Flag (flag, never auto-reject)
Older companies likely sitting on old/legacy codebases get a **flag**, not a rejection. He can still review them. He does not want to spend his time maintaining old, crappy software. Signal-words: "maintain our established platform," "modernize legacy systems" (mixed — could be a rewrite, which is good), COBOL/AS400/on-prem-only stacks, decades-old enterprise vendors. Note the signal so he can judge.

### Priority Signals (boost ranking)
- Newer companies, greenfield applications, newer products.
- AI-assisted development / AI tooling adoption as a real engineering practice (not just a customer-facing LLM feature — see the fit-trap screen).
- Frontend / UI architecture ownership, cross-platform, component-library / design-system work.
- End-to-end ownership (business relationship → architecture → implementation → deploy → maintain).
- Fintech / financial-data domain (he has real experience here, it's a plus — but he's open to almost anything).

### Experience Required
- **Hard reject:** roles requiring 7 or more years of experience. Roles requiring under 3 or less years of experience.

### Role Type
- **Hard reject:** backend-only roles (no frontend responsibility).
- **Accept:** frontend or full-stack roles. Full-stack must include real frontend ownership, not UI as a nice-to-have.

### Fit-Trap Screen (judgment — see cover-letter-system for full detail)
Flag or down-rank roles that look attractive but are a substance mismatch:
- Backend-systems / SRE / infra / billing / distributed-systems roles wearing nice culture language.
- "Full-stack" that's really backend-heavy (UI is "nice to have").
- AI as a *customer feature* (RAG, prompt-eng, LLM product) rather than an internal dev practice.
- Very senior title, very junior IC scope.
- Role-evolution bait ("could grow into architecture/leadership" but the actual job is narrow).
